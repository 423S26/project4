What is our goal?

To create a webpage where Indigenous peoples can share stories, language, and history.

Learn & Translate

History & Treaties

Learn about Crow history, important leaders, and treaty agreements that shaped the nation.

About Us

We are Leon Birdhat and Luka Derry, students at Montana State University. Leon is a Computer Science major with a minor in Native Studies. Luka is a Computer Science major with a minor in U.S. History.

This project was created to support Indigenous language preservation and cultural education, with a special focus on the Crow Nation (Apsáalooke). We hope this site serves as a respectful space for learning, sharing stories, and honoring history.
