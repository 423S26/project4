About Us

We are Leon Birdhat and Luka Derry, students at Montana State University. Leon is a Computer Science major with a minor in Native Studies. Luka is a Computer Science major with a minor in U.S. History.

Our Mission

This project supports Indigenous language preservation and cultural education across Montana's tribal nations. We want this to be a respectful, community-driven place for sharing stories and honoring living traditions.
