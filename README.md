Native Language & History – Montana Tribal Nations
Live Website: https://project4original-zeta.vercel.app/
A respectful, community-driven digital platform dedicated to preserving Montana’s Indigenous languages, educating about tribal histories and treaties, and sharing stories from Montana’s 12 tribal nations across 7 reservations.
Created by:

Leon Birdhat – Computer Science major with a minor in Native Studies
Luka Derry – Computer Science major with a minor in U.S. History

Both students at Montana State University, Bozeman, Montana.
Mission
To support Indigenous language revitalization and cultural education by providing accessible resources on languages, treaty history, sovereignty, and community stories.

Site Structure & Features
Homepage

Powerful hero section with the tagline: “Preserving. Learning. Honoring Montana’s First Languages.”
Key statistics about Montana’s tribal nations.
Inspirational quote by Marcus Garvey.
Three clickable feature cards leading to Learn, History, and Stories.

Learn (html/learn.html)

Hero section encouraging users to explore tribal nations.
Tribe Selector Grid featuring all 8 nations with:
English name + Indigenous name (e.g., Crow Nation / Apsáalooke)
Representative images
Status: “Language hub in development”

New Feature (March 2026): Interactive Plains Indian Sign Language (PISL) modal.
Trigger: “Watch PISL Demonstration” box.
Opens a centered modal embedding the video: James Wooden Legs – ASL vs PISL
Auto-plays on open, fully stops on close, accessible (keyboard + click-outside support).


Future plans: Full language hubs with vocabulary, audio, phrases, and translation tools for each nation.
History (html/culture.html)

In-depth exploration of treaties that shaped Montana’s tribal nations (Fort Laramie 1851/1868, Hellgate 1855, Lame Bull 1855, etc.).
Profiles of tribal leaders and resilience.
Explanations of sovereignty, reserved rights (hunting, fishing, water), and impacts of allotment policies.
Embedded Native Land Digital interactive map for educational purposes.
Section on modern cultural resilience.

Stories

Stories Overview (html/stories.html): Introduction + links to browse or submit stories.
Community Submissions (html/community.html):
Dynamic, filterable grid of user-submitted stories.
Filters by Tribe, Category (Landscapes, Lineage, Creation Story, Personal, Community, etc.), and sorting options.
Stories load live from a Google Sheet.
Each card includes title, description, tags, and link to full story (PDF/file).


Share Your Story: Submit via the Google Form. Submissions are reviewed before publication.

How to Contribute

Submit a Story: Use the form on the Stories page.
Language Resources: Share vocabulary, audio recordings, phrases, or other materials via email.
Feedback & Ideas: We welcome respectful suggestions to improve the site.

Respectful Use
This project aims to honor Montana’s tribal nations. All content is shared with the intention of supporting language preservation and cultural education. Please use the site and its resources with care and respect.

Thank you for visiting and supporting this work. Every contribution helps keep Montana’s Indigenous languages and histories alive for future generations.
